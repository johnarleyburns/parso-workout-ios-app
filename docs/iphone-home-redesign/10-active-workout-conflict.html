Two gates, one rule

Tap Start on Home and show this decision immediately. Also enforce the same rule at the final launch boundary for Quick Start, Coach, Previous, Plan, History reuse, cardio, swim, HIIT, boxing, and any future deep link. The second gate prevents double taps and route races.

Cancel is recoverable

Cancel Previous Workout… opens a destructive confirmation naming the workout and its logged-set count. Confirming soft-deletes a strength session (restorable from History) or runs the modality’s explicit discard/stop cleanup, then continues the pending start intent. Never create the replacement first.

Resume wins safely

Resume discards the pending start intent and presents the existing workout with its original clock, sets, and sensor state. Not Now changes nothing. Buttons remain idempotent under rapid or repeated taps.

Global meaning

“One workout” includes live strength, outdoor/timer cardio, swim, and interval sessions started on iPhone. Manual past logging is not a live workout and remains available. A single coordinator/lease owns this truth; individual screens may not infer it from presentation state.
