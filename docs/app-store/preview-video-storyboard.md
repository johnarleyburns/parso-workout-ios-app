# App Store preview video storyboard

This is the capture brief for the final device-recorded preview; no video is
claimed to exist until it is recorded on a real iPhone and Watch.

1. 0–3 s — Today opens on the completed/next-workout state in light mode.
2. 3–7 s — hold the next set’s **Log** button, speak “57 kilos for 8,” and
   release; show the immediate saved set and the Undo chip.
3. 7–11 s — swipe to the partner row, show the performer-specific quick log
   target, and tap Repeat.
4. 11–15 s — open This Week, tap the enlarged muscle-map target once, and show
   the detail sheet.
5. 15–20 s — show the Progress zoom transition and a PR takeover, then the
   live-workout bottom accessory returning to the session.
6. 20–25 s — show Watch strength logging and the Apple Health/export privacy
   surface.

Capture both light and dark UI evidence, use realistic but non-identifying data,
disable debug overlays, and verify every spoken result before recording. Deliver
the final H.264/HEVC asset in the App Store Connect-supported resolution plus a
caption/transcript file.
