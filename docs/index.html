Cladiron

Strength and cardio training for iPhone and Apple Watch · Parso Consulting

Cladiron is an open-source strength and cardio training app. It needs no account, contains no tracking or advertising, and stores your history only on your device. A full JSON archive is the backup and device-migration path.
