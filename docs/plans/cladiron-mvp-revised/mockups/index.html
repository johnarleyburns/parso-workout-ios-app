What changed from v1.0

• iPhone gains full trainer capability — a coach can author a complete plan, set for set, on a phone.
• New compact authoring model: set stack + chip rows, the prescription bar, set schemes, shorthand entry, bulk apply.
• iPad is a full-fidelity trainer workbench: menu bar, keyboard, pointer, multi-window.
• v2.1: the Mac app is kept and native, but no longer a separate product — same bundle ID, same App Store record, Universal Purchase.
• Cladiron Pro is the only purchase and gates clients alone; 30-day trial; one purchase covers iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
• All self-coached use is free forever on every surface, including the entire expert system and partner sessions.
• v2.3: Cladiron is proprietary and closed source; the exercise database, annotations, and related DB++ tooling remain open for other apps.
• v2.4: trainers can coach clients on any platform with privacy-reviewed HTML/PDF/text plan packets shared through Mail, Messages, WeChat, or another installed app, plus explicit manual result capture.
• v2.5: planning is layered onto the shipped, field-tested strength/cardio/Watch runtime. The 20-muscle DB++ model, set-entry ergonomics, partner defaults/rotation, HealthKit, and durable Watch sync are preservation contracts.
