HIIT & Boxing — the color-coded timer

The whole screen is the signal, readable across the room; color + label + icon + haptics carry meaning. Reuses IntervalRunner.

Phase 1

Live heart rate — your source, your choice

High-frequency watch-native stream or a Bluetooth chest strap paired to the watch. Never the slow Fitness-app samples.

Phase 0 · 3

Launch & standalone strength logging

Start from a Smart-Start routine or resume a handoff, then log sets/reps/weight on the wrist. Syncs to the phone by UUID.

Phase 4

Partners & smart swaps — the differentiators

Rotate the logger through a partner roster after each set; swap a broken/occupied machine for a ranked substitute — no keyboard needed.

Phase 5

Handoff, summary & the Smart Stack

Move a live session between devices, wrap up with a summary, and glance at the current phase from the watch face.

Reliability, by design. Every screen above runs on shared, swift test-covered logic from CadenceCore/CadenceFeatures. The watch is embedded in the phone archive (the fix for why it never shipped), logs to its own local store, and syncs by UUID — no CloudKit on the wrist. Verified on TestFlight, with a single on-device smoke on one named simulator.
