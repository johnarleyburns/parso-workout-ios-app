# Cladiron press kit

## One sentence

Cladiron is a free, open-source, privacy-first strength coach that explains
recommendations, logs real training history, and works with iPhone, Apple Watch,
Apple Health, JSON, and FIT.

## 100 words

Cladiron helps self-coached lifters decide what to do next and understand why.
It records fast strength and cardio sessions, preserves actual loads and reps,
shows PR and volume context, and builds plans with published sport-science
citations. Partner workouts keep each person’s history separate. Apple Watch can
run strength, interval, and cardio sessions phone-free, while Apple Health
provides import and summary backup. The complete record remains portable through
lossless JSON and supported FIT exchange. There is no account, advertising,
telemetry, or developer-operated data service. Cladiron is free; an optional
contribution only adds a Supporter badge.

## Founder note

“The gym interaction should feel immediate, while the reasoning should remain
inspectable. Cladiron is built around that balance: useful coaching without a
black box, and a complete training record the lifter can export.”
