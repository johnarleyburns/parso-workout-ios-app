Cladiron 2.3

Licensing

Cladiron is proprietary, closed-source software owned by Parso Consulting. All rights are reserved.

Open foundation

Cladiron is a proprietary, privacy-first application built on the open free-exercise-db-plusplus project. The exercise database, annotations, and related tooling remain freely available for use by other applications.

Privacy by design

No tracking, no ads, and no developer-operated server. Training data stays on your devices and in your private iCloud. Export remains available at any time.
