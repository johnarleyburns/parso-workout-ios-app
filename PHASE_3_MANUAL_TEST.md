# Phase 3 Manual Field Test

Phase 3 is now the individual-workout depth and optional Supporter slice. The
retired weekly Plan tab, weekly coach generation, Plan depth, and Review with
Coach screens are not part of this test.

## 1. Personalized and custom workout depth

1. Confirm existing history is present after installing the candidate build.
2. Open Start Workout → Suggest a Workout. Confirm it opens one editable
   Personalized Workout directly.
3. Open the exercise info button in edit and view modes. Confirm the Volume
   section shows primary muscles with a full green bar and partial muscles with
   a half yellow bar.
4. Exclude one suggested exercise. Confirm the current suggested workout is
   regenerated without it. Open Settings → Excluded Exercises, verify the
   reason, and remove the exclusion.
5. Open Custom Workout. Browse first, then Recents and Popular. Search by
   canonical name, equipment, and primary muscle. Confirm muscle search returns
   only exercises where that muscle is primary and shows primary-focus percent.
6. Open substitution from a plan and an active workout. Confirm similarity
   percentages are visible, newer substitutions remain authoritative, and an
   older substitute cannot overwrite the current choice.
7. Check rep ladders such as 12-10-8 and 12-10-8-6. Confirm repeated ladders
   such as 12-12-12 are not generated for normal strength prescriptions.

## 2. Volume, history, and execution

1. Confirm Workout Plan, active Workout, Workout Summary, and historical
   Summary each show the Volume section.
2. Confirm plan/active volume is sorted by descending sets, then alphabetically,
   and omits zero-set muscles. Confirm the weekly background contribution is
   distinct from the current-workout total.
3. Save a set and confirm Home and live-workout volume update without blocking
   set entry. Check fractional historical loads are repeated exactly rather than
   rounded.
4. Open an exercise previously performed, enter Set Entry, and confirm its
   history is present there as well as in the workout row.

## 3. Optional Supporter contribution

1. Open Settings → Support Cladiron.
2. Confirm the only product is the optional $9.99 contribution. There is no
   Pro product, trial, paywall, or feature gating.
3. After a successful purchase, confirm the Home Supporter badge appears and
   remains after relaunch. Confirm all core features remain available without
   purchasing.

## 4. Record results

Record build/device details and failures with screenshots and timestamps. Phase
3 field review is complete when personalized/custom planning, exclusion/search,
   substitution, volume/history, and optional contribution behavior pass.
