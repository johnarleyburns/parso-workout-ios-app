Cladiron · watchOS · v3 plan

Watch-only fitness — screen mockups

Every screen for the v3 watch app: the four field-reported fixes (live HR, frozen interval clock, kg-only lifts, no save/cancel) plus the full cardio suite — Run, Walk, Cycle indoor & outdoor, Swim with laps, Rowing, and Other — with partner rotation, auto-pause, and configurable HIIT/Boxing rounds, so a week of training needs no phone. All decisions settled 2026-07-17. Grouped by build phase; FIX marks repaired v2 screens, NEW marks screens that don't exist yet.

W1

Make it live — launcher, HR & the interval clock

Auth gate fixed so the workout session actually starts; the countdown is driven by the timeline; every finished workout is now saved to HealthKit (rings + phone ingest).

W2

Units — pounds on the wrist

lb/kg preference on the watch: seeded from locale (US → lb), overwritten by the phone's setting via applicationContext, editable on the watch and synced back.

W3

Strength — the normal workout lifecycle, with partners

Renamed from “Lift”. Session home hub with Finish & Save and Cancel always reachable; warm-up sets, cool-down timer, partner rotation (settled D4), and a summary — mirroring the iPhone flow's semantics.

W4

Cardio — run, walk, cycle, swim, rowing, other

Apple-Workout grammar: setup → metrics page, swipe left for controls. Distance, pace and energy stream from the live workout builder; outdoor sessions auto-pause (settled D7); HIIT/Boxing gain a rounds/work/rest setup (settled D8). Workouts save to HealthKit and reach the phone through the existing ingest.
