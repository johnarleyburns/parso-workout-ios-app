Cladiron Privacy Policy

Cladiron (bundle id guru.parso.ios-workout-app) · Parso Consulting · Last updated November 4, 2026

Cladiron runs entirely on your device, requires no account, and sends none of your training data to us.

1. Privacy principles

Cladiron is a free, open-source strength and cardio training app. It needs no account, contains no tracking or advertising, and its source code is public.

2. Information we collect

Your workout logs, strength and cardio assessments, coaching insights, and preferences are stored only on your device (and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud account — see section 5). None of it is sent to us.

3. Information we do not collect

No accounts, no personal identifiers, no analytics or telemetry, no advertising SDKs, and no device fingerprinting.

4. Apple Health

With your permission, Cladiron reads steps, workouts, heart rate, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, and body weight from Health, to show your activity and recovery-aware coaching. It writes summaries of your strength, cardio, interval, and swim workouts (plus heart-rate samples and GPS routes) back to Health so they appear system-wide and count toward your Activity rings. Every permission can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings, and this data stays on your device or in your own iCloud — never sent to us.

5. iCloud backup & sync

Optional, off by default. When enabled, your data syncs through your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. We have no access to it.

6. Bluetooth heart-rate sensors

If you connect a Bluetooth chest strap, readings go directly from your sensor to your device and are stored locally. They are never sent to us.

7. Location

Optional, used only to map and measure outdoor runs and rides you record. Location data stays on your device and can be disabled at any time in iOS Settings.

8. Microphone & voice logging

Cladiron can use the microphone so you can log a set hands-free ("Quick Talk" on iPhone, "Log set" on Apple Watch). Your speech is transcribed to text — on-device when your iPhone supports on-device speech recognition, or otherwise using Apple's server-based speech recognition — so you can review the transcription before anything is logged. The audio recording itself is deleted once transcription finishes. This feature only activates when you choose to use it.

9. Data sharing

We don't share your data with third parties, because we don't collect it in the first place. The one exception, described fully below, is Apple's own platform services (Health, iCloud, Bluetooth, Speech, and the App Store) acting entirely on your device and under your own Apple ID — never a service we operate.

10. Beta testing (TestFlight)

If you test Cladiron through Apple's TestFlight and choose to share crash reports with developers (an iOS setting you control), Apple may pass us a sanitized crash log so we can fix the bug. This is Apple's own standard TestFlight feature, governed by Apple's own privacy terms — Cladiron includes no separate analytics or crash-reporting code of its own, and this only applies during beta testing, never to the released App Store app.

11. In-app purchase

The optional, one-time "Supporter" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. It unlocks no feature — it's a tip jar. We never see your payment details; Apple handles the transaction the same way it does for every App Store purchase.

12. Security & control

There is no central server to breach, because there is no server. Your data lives in Cladiron's sandboxed app storage, protected by iOS's own encryption. You can delete individual entries at any time, or delete the app to remove everything.

13. Not medical advice

Cladiron's assessments and coaching recommendations are general training guidance based on published research — not medical advice. Consult a doctor before starting a new training program, especially if you have a health condition.

14. Children's privacy

Cladiron does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information from anyone.

15. App Store compliance

Cladiron does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so no App Tracking Transparency prompt is required. Only Apple-provided frameworks (HealthKit, CloudKit, CoreBluetooth, CoreLocation, Speech, StoreKit) are used — no third-party SDKs.

16. Open source

Cladiron's complete source code is public, under the GNU General Public License v3.0 or later with the Cladiron App Store Exception (which permits distribution through the Apple App Store and TestFlight), at github.com/johnarleyburns/parso-workout-ios-app. The Cladiron name, icon, and other brand assets are not covered by this license.

17. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be posted both here and in the app. Continuing to use Cladiron after a change means you accept the update.
